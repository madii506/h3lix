09 — Docs

man h3lix

How the agent works, what it never does, and the honest limits.

NAME

h3lix — an autonomous agent that pays $H3LIX holders through Solana Privacy Rings.

CYCLE

Hourly, on the hour (UTC): claim creator fees from paired coins → deposit into the Ring → snapshot holders and spirals → one private transfer per batch of up to 15 holders → sync and publish.

WEIGHT

weight = balance × spiral. spiral = 1 + min(days held, 30) / 15. Selling any amount resets days held to 0.

PRIVATE WALLET

Your wallet signs the Solana off-chain message TSPP/derive/v1. The Privacy Rings SDK turns that signature into your shielded keys, in this tab. The keys are never sent anywhere; only the public shielded address is stored, in this browser.

REGISTRATION

A Ring transfer can only reach an owner who published a shielded address onchain. Holders who never do are paid as a plain public withdrawal instead.

H3LIX NEVER

asks for a seed phrase or private key · moves funds from your wallet · signs for you · shows a number it did not read.

LIMITS

Privacy Rings is alpha software on devnet. Private payouts start when it runs on mainnet; until then the agent pays nothing in its name. Ring transfers hide amount and asset, not who sent or received.
