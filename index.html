02 — The cycle

One cycle, every hour.

On the hour the agent claims what paired coins earned, shields it, and pays every holder by weight. Then it syncs its private wallet and waits for the next hour.

03 — The spiral

The longer you hold, the bigger your cut.

Your share of a cycle is your balance times your spiral. It starts at 1× and climbs every day you hold, up to 3× at day 30. Sell any and it starts over.

08 — FAQ

Questions

What is H3LIX?

An autonomous agent. It collects creator fees from coins paired to $H3LIX, shields them in a Solana Privacy Ring and pays $H3LIX holders every cycle.

Is it part of Helius?

No. H3LIX is independent. It uses Privacy Rings, the shielded pool Helius publishes as an open SDK.

How do I get paid in private?

Open the terminal, connect your wallet and sign one message. That derives your private wallet; the agent pays it through the Ring.

What is the spiral?

Your multiplier. It grows from 1× to 3× over 30 days of holding and resets when you sell.

How does a coin pair to $H3LIX?

pump.fun lets a coin trade against another coin instead of SOL. A paired coin quotes in $H3LIX and shares its creator fees to the agent.

Can the agent touch my wallet?

No. The site only asks you to connect and sign. Your keys and the derived private keys stay in your browser.

Why is the Ring on devnet?

Privacy Rings is in alpha and runs on devnet first. Payouts switch to private as Helius brings it to mainnet; until then nothing is paid in its name.
